Delete your aiOn account

Sign in with the Apple ID or Google account you use for aiOn. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is deleted.

Deleting your aiOn account:

  • stops the account working straight away, and signs it out on your phone;
  • removes your profile, settings, health profile and sign-in details from aiOn's service within 30 days;
  • releases your rings, so someone else can pair them;
  • can be undone for 30 days: sign in to the aiOn app again and choose Restore my account.
Your Apple ID or Google account is not deleted or changed. aiOn only stops having access to it.
