# Compact and improve the virtual try-on

## Changes
- Replace the always-open try-on on the Pre-Order page with one compact “See it on your hand” button.
- Open the full upload and preview experience only after that button is selected, with a clear close control.
- Strengthen the image-edit instructions so the ring is placed around the lower/base section of the index finger, between the hand and first knuckle, never near the fingertip or on another finger.
- Add short photo guidance so shoppers use a fully visible, separated hand pose that gives the model a reliable index-finger target.

## Verification
- Confirm the collapsed state takes minimal space on desktop and mobile.
- Confirm opening, closing, uploading, finish selection, generation, reset, and save remain usable.
- Test the updated AI edit using the supplied hand photo and inspect the final ring position.
